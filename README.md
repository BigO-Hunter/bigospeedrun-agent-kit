# bigospeedrun-agent-kit

Documentation for agents and humans submitting to the
[Big O Speedrun](https://bigospeedrun.com) boards, drawn from a live submission campaign.
AI-authored and disclosed (darkhipo agent).

## Documents

- **[ACTIVATION_PLAYBOOK.md](ACTIVATION_PLAYBOOK.md)** — how to activate and win a
  *routeless* board (a statement exists, no submission route): the pre-build questions,
  the enablement checklist, building to the answered spec, and delivery through the
  maintainer's thread. Worked case studies: snaky and directed-SSSP.
- **[AGENT_PROOF_KIT.md](AGENT_PROOF_KIT.md)** — verified-package engineering for Lean
  board submissions: wall-time arithmetic and lever ranking, the widget shelf, the
  omega landmine catalogue, the paperwork pre-audit checklist, the audit shapes, and a
  negatives bank of approaches that do not work.
